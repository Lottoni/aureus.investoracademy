The 5-Step
Investor Method

Stock tips are easy to find. Knowing what you're buying is harder. Here is how Aureus takes you from curious to confident, one step at a time.

1

Learn what makes a good business

Learn what makes a good business and the key things to check before buying, with every finance term explained in one sentence.

2

Pick a company on your own

Find a company you're curious about on your own, not from a hype video.

3

See how it really works

The app breaks down the company's official reports into simple sections: how it makes money, its competition and its risks.

4

Find out what it's worth

Find out what the company is actually worth and whether today's price makes sense.

5

Make your own call

Get a professionally formatted investment thesis and make your own call. The decision is always yours.

For parents

Aureus is a learning tool. It doesn't place real trades or connect to a brokerage account. Students practice with a virtual portfolio and can track what they own. It never tells anyone what to buy.

The story

Built by a student,
for students

I'm Luca, a finance student at Concordia's John Molson School of Business in Montreal. It started because I really wanted to join the investing club at school. Over the summer I read The Intelligent Investor, and to make sure I didn't forget it, I built a website to put it into practice.

Then I noticed people my age picking stocks from TikTok without understanding the company, and I couldn't find anything that taught people to invest while making them practice it. So I built Aureus.
